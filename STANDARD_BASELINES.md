# Standard / Regulation Baselines

Verification date: 2026-09-18

This file records official source baselines used by the product UI. It is metadata only; it does not reproduce copyrighted standards text.

## IFRS / IAS
- Official source: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/
- 2026 Required edition contains IFRS Accounting Standards and amendments required for periods beginning on 1 January 2026.
- Questions must still carry their own as-of date because later-issued amendments can have later effective dates.

## US GAAP
- Official source: https://asc.fasb.org/
- FASB Accounting Standards Codification is the single official source of authoritative nongovernmental US GAAP.
- Effective dates must be checked for applicable ASUs.

## IAASB / ISA
- Official source: https://www.iaasb.org/standards-pronouncements
- Current-pronouncements page is the baseline for auditing and quality-management material.
- Do not assume every newly published standard is effective in every jurisdiction on publication date.

## Saudi Arabia / SOCPA
- Official endorsement page: https://socpa.org.sa/Socpa/Pages/Knowledge-Center/101.aspx?lang=en-us
- Use the Saudi endorsement/adoption documents when the user selects Saudi jurisdiction.

## Saudi Arabia / ZATCA
- Official regulations: https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Pages/default.aspx
- Tax/zakat questions require Saudi jurisdiction plus explicit as-of date.

## Sultanate of Oman / Tax Authority
- VAT law and regulations: https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/vat-law-regulations
- The portal lists the VAT Law, Executive Regulations, and subsequent amending decisions.
- Oman tax questions require Oman jurisdiction plus explicit as-of date.

## AAOIFI
- Electronic standards: https://aaoifi.com/e-standards/?lang=en
- Use for Islamic-finance accounting/auditing/governance training where relevant.

## IPSAS
- Official source: https://www.ipsasb.org/standards-pronouncements
- Use for public-sector accounting training, subject to local adoption rules.

## Citation rule
A model-generated paragraph number is never treated as authoritative solely because the model supplied it. The product should display an exact paragraph only when the verification layer has high confidence; otherwise it must leave the paragraph blank and direct the learner to the official source.
